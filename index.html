How to play
  1. Place one in every row and every column.
  2. Each colored patch gets exactly one .
  3. No two can touch, not even diagonally.

Tap a cell once to mark it with ×, twice to place a , and a third time to clear it. Pieces that break a rule get a red outline.

Drag across cells to mark several with × at once. Start the drag on a × to erase them instead.

Each level has one puzzle a day, the same for everyone. The clock starts on your first move.
