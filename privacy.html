Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Patchwork Pals is a free daily logic puzzle at interaminense.dev/patchwork-pals. This page explains what data the site uses, why, and the choices you have.

No accounts, no personal data collected by us

You can play without signing up. The site has no server of its own and we don't collect your name, email address or any other personal information.

What is saved on your device

To keep your progress, the game stores a few small items in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and we can't see them:

  • patchwork-pals-daily-v1: today's date, the board you're working on, your time, hints used and which levels you've solved.
  • patchwork-pals-seen-help: whether you've already seen the "How to play" window.

You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Doing so resets today's progress.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads that help keep the game free. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance, including ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google's Ad Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you'll be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Other services the site relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it may log technical data such as your IP address when you load a page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google's servers to download them. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Sharing: the "Post on X" button opens X with a prepared message, and anything you post there follows X's privacy policy. "Copy result" and the Instagram card only work on your device until you choose to paste or post them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as Brazil's LGPD and the EU's GDPR give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. Since we don't store personal data ourselves, the quickest way to remove what's on your device is to clear this site's data in your browser. For data held by Google or GitHub, use the links above.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but it isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/interaminense/patchwork-pals/issues.
